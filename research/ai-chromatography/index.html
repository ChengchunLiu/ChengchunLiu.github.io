Automation and AI-Powered Prediction in Chromatographic Separation

Chengchun Liu and Fanyang Mo

A research perspective connecting automated experimentation, retention prediction, condition optimization, and closed-loop chromatographic separation.

10.1021/acs.accounts.5c00677 ↗
04 · AI Chromatography
Molecule Column Closed loop

01 / Problem

Problem

Separation development connects molecular interactions, stationary phases, instruments, and operating conditions, yet these layers are often optimized separately through iterative experimentation.

02 / Approach

Approach

The perspective organizes the field across scales, linking automated experiments, retention prediction, condition optimization, and closed-loop decision making.

03 / Why it matters

Why it matters

It shows how predictive models and experimental automation can be treated as one connected workflow rather than isolated tools.
