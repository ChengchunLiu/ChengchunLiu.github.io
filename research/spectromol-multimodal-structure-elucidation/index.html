Angewandte Chemie International Edition · 2026

Hypothesis-and-Refinement Learning of Organic Structures From Multimodal Spectroscopic Data

Chengchun Liu†, Zhiyuan Yan†, Li Yuan, Hao Li, Boxuan Zhao, Yonghong Tian, Bartosz A. Grzybowski, and Fanyang Mo

A hypothesis-and-refinement system that proposes molecular structures from multimodal spectra, then repairs them with formula-, exact-mass-, and unsaturation-aware molecular generation.

10.1002/anie.9212238 ↗
01 · SpectroMol
NMR + IR + MS Hypothesis Refinement

01 / Problem

Problem

Molecular structure elucidation is an underdetermined inverse problem: each spectrum contains only partial evidence, while many structures can appear plausible.

02 / Approach

Approach

The workflow first proposes candidate structures from complementary spectral evidence, then refines them using explicit molecular-formula, exact-mass, and unsaturation constraints.

03 / Why it matters

Why it matters

Combining multimodal evidence with explicit chemical constraints makes automated structure elucidation more chemically grounded and easier to inspect.
