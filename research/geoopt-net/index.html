Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation · 2026

A Cross-Domain Graph Learning Protocol for Single-Step Molecular Geometry Refinement

Chengchun Liu, Wendi Cai, Boxuan Zhao, and Fanyang Mo

GeoOpt-Net uses SE(3)-equivariant, multifidelity learning to map inexpensive force-field conformers directly toward DFT-quality molecular geometries in one deterministic forward pass.

10.1021/acs.jctc.6c01080 ↗ View source code↗
02 · GeoOpt-Net
Force field SE(3) DFT geometry

01 / Problem

Problem

Reliable quantum-chemical calculations require accurate molecular geometries, but iterative DFT optimization becomes costly when many molecules must be processed.

02 / Approach

Approach

An SE(3)-equivariant graph architecture and multifidelity learning map an inexpensive initial conformer toward a target quantum-chemical geometry in one deterministic forward pass.

03 / Why it matters

Why it matters

A direct refinement step can reduce the geometry-preparation burden in large-scale quantum-chemical workflows while respecting the geometric symmetries of the task.
